Saintpaulia Studio is built by a solo developer. Your feedback directly shapes what gets built next.

## Report a bug

A useful bug report includes what you were trying to do, what happened instead, steps to reproduce the issue, your device and browser, and a screenshot if possible.

To report a bug, email [support@saintpauliastudio.com](mailto:support@saintpauliastudio.com?subject=Bug%20Report) with the subject line **Bug Report**.

## Request a feature

The most helpful feature requests describe what you want to do that you currently can't, why it matters for your workflow, and roughly how you imagine it working.

To request a feature, email [support@saintpauliastudio.com](mailto:support@saintpauliastudio.com?subject=Feature%20Request) with the subject line **Feature Request**.

## General feedback

Whether it's a compliment, a frustration, or a passing thought about the app — we read everything. Even brief feedback helps with prioritization.

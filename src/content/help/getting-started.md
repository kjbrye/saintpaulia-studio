This guide walks you from account creation to your first logged care action.

## Create your account

Visit [saintpauliastudio.com](https://saintpauliastudio.com) and sign up with your email address. You'll receive a confirmation email — click the link to verify and you're in.

## The dashboard

After logging in, you land on your **Dashboard** — designed to answer "what needs my attention today?" and then let you linger. It opens with a **Today** section of task cards for the care that's due, so the most time-sensitive work is one tap away. Below that:

- **Care overview** — an at-a-glance summary of how much of your collection is up to date.
- **Collection at a glance** — running counts of plants, propagations, crosses, and sports.
- **Recent activity** — the latest care logs and updates across your collection.
- **A sanctuary moment** — a quiet, rotating note to close the page on something calmer than a to-do list.

If your collection is empty, you'll see a prompt to add your first violet instead.

## Adding your first plant

Tap **Add Plant** from your dashboard or library page. The key fields:

**Cultivar name** is the registered variety name (e.g., "Optimara EverGrace", "Rob's Vanilla Trail"). This is the primary identifier. If you don't know the variety, use NOID.

**Nickname** is optional — a personal name you use for the plant ("Kitchen Shelf Vi", "Mom's Gift"). If set, it displays in place of the cultivar name in most views. Helpful when you have multiples of the same variety.

You can also upload a **photo** and fill in details like pot size, acquisition date, and source. Once saved, your plant appears in the **Plant Library**.

## Logging care

Open any plant's detail page and you'll see quick-action buttons for the five care types:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Water | Logs a watering event, updates "last watered" date |
| Fertilize | Logs fertilizing, lets you select the fertilizer type |
| Groom | Logs grooming (removing dead leaves, spent blooms) |
| Repot | Logs a repotting event, lets you update pot size |
| Treatment | Logs a pest or disease treatment, lets you select the treatment type |

Each tap creates a timestamped entry in the **Care Log** and updates the dashboard. The app is designed for mobile use — log care at the shelf, not from memory later.

## Viewing care history

The **Care Log** page shows all logged actions across your collection. Filter by plant or care type to find specific records, or switch to the calendar view to see your care history laid out by day.

## Next steps

Once you're comfortable with the basics, browse the **Guides** for a deeper look at each capability, or jump to the **Breeding and propagation workflows** if you're working with crosses.

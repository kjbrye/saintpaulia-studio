Analytics features help you understand your collection at a higher level — spotting trends, tracking bloom performance, and identifying what's working in your care routine.

## Dashboard

The dashboard provides a snapshot of your collection: how many plants need attention (overdue care in at least one category), how many are healthy with all care up to date, and running counts across plants, propagations, crosses, and sports. It answers the question "what needs my attention today?"

## Bloom logging and statistics

*Coming soon.* Bloom logging will let you record when each plant blooms, including bloom color, form, and duration. Over time this builds into statistics that reveal which plants bloom most frequently, seasonal patterns across your collection, and whether care changes correlate with better bloom performance.

## Care analytics

*Coming soon.* Care analytics will aggregate your care log data to surface insights like average care frequency per plant, fertilizer usage patterns, and which plants consistently get neglected.

## Data export

You can export your plant data, care logs, bloom logs, health logs, journal entries, propagations, and breeding records at any time from **Settings → Data**, for backup or analysis in external tools.

Check the **Roadmap** for updates on when the upcoming insights become available.

The Breeding Tracker guides you through the entire cross-pollination lifecycle, from selecting parent plants to harvesting seeds and evaluating offspring. For step-by-step walkthroughs, see the **Breeding and propagation workflows**.

## Terminology

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Cross | A breeding attempt between two plants, written with the botanical × symbol |
| Pod parent | The plant that receives pollen and develops the seed pod |
| Pollen parent | The plant that provides pollen |
| F1 generation | First-generation offspring from a cross |
| F2 generation | Offspring from crossing two F1 plants (or selfing an F1) |

## Creating a cross

Both plants should be in active bloom. Navigate to the Breeding Tracker, tap **New Cross**, and select the pod parent and pollen parent from your library. The cross is automatically labeled using standard notation: `Pod Parent × Pollen Parent`.

## Breeding stages

Each cross progresses through six stages. At each stage, a **Stage Advance** modal captures the date and stage-specific data.

| Stage | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Pollinated | Date attempted, bloom condition, technique notes. Watch for the ovary swelling |
| Pod forming | Progress and any concerns (pod dropping, slow growth). Typically 6–9 months until ripe |
| Harvested | Harvest date, seed count if known. Sow within 1–2 weeks |
| Sown | Medium, date, conditions (light, temperature, humidity). 3–8 weeks to germinate |
| Sprouted | Germination rate and date of first sprouts. 8–14 months to first bloom |
| Blooming | Trait assessment once seedlings flower and can be evaluated |

At each stage advance you can add notes that are saved to a **stage log**, creating a chronological journal of the entire breeding process. Record environmental conditions, bloom quality, and technique details — this data is invaluable over time.

## Cross statuses

**Active** — The cross is in progress through the stages above.

**Completed** — Offspring have been evaluated. The app prompts you to add them to your collection.

**Failed** — The cross did not succeed (pollination didn't take, pod dropped, seeds didn't germinate).

**Archived** — Kept for records but not actively being worked on.

## Adding offspring

When a cross is marked as Completed, the app launches the **Add Offspring** flow. It pre-fills the pod parent, pollen parent, generation label (F1, F2, etc.), and cross reference so your new plants are immediately connected to their breeding history in the lineage system. Because a cross produces genetically new plants, these appear as descendants in the lineage tree.

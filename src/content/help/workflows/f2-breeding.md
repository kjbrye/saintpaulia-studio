The second generation is where hybridizing gets interesting — hidden recessive traits can finally surface.

## Cross two F1 plants

To create F2 offspring, cross two F1 plants from the same or a related original cross (or self a single F1). Set the cross up in the Breeding Tracker the same way as any other — select the pod and pollen parents from your library.

## Let the app label the generation

The app automatically labels the offspring as **F2** based on the parents' generations. You don't need to set it by hand; the generation is derived from the lineage you've already recorded.

## Expect variation

F2 crosses are where you often see the most trait variation. Recessive traits that were masked in the F1 generation can emerge here, so evaluate seedlings carefully and keep notes — this is where new and unexpected results tend to show up.

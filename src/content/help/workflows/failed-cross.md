Not every cross succeeds — and a failed cross is still useful data.

## Record what happened

Open the cross in the Breeding Tracker and add notes about what went wrong at whatever stage the failure occurred: the pod dropped, nothing germinated, the seedlings died. The more detail you capture, the more useful the record is later.

## Mark it Failed

Change the cross status to **Failed**. It moves out of your active crosses but stays on record, with its full stage log intact.

## Learn from it

Failed crosses accumulate into a picture of what does and doesn't work in your program. Over time you can review which parent combinations tend to fail and adjust your breeding strategy accordingly.

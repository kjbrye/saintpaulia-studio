This walkthrough takes a single cross from choosing parents all the way to adding the offspring to your collection.

## Choose your parents

Before you start in the app, decide which two plants you want to cross. Consider bloom color, leaf type, growth habit, and any traits you're hoping to combine. Both plants should be in active bloom.

## Create the cross in the app

Go to the Breeding Tracker, tap **New Cross**, and select the pod parent (the one that will carry the seed pod) and the pollen parent (the one providing pollen). The cross is automatically named `Pod Parent × Pollen Parent`.

## Pollinate

Physically pollinate the pod parent, then advance the cross to the **Pollinated** stage in the app. Record the date and any notes about technique or bloom condition. Over the next week or two, watch for the ovary to swell — the first sign the cross has taken.

## Wait for the pod to form

Advance to **Pod forming**. African violet seed pods typically take 6–9 months to ripen. Add notes as needed. If the pod drops before maturing, mark the cross as Failed and record why.

## Harvest

When the pod is dry and beginning to split, harvest it and advance to **Harvested**. Record the date and, if you can, a rough seed count. Sow within a week or two.

## Sow the seeds

Sow on your preferred medium and advance to **Sown**. Note the medium, date, and conditions (light, temperature, humidity). Seeds usually germinate in 3–8 weeks.

## Grow the seedlings on

As seedlings emerge, advance to **Sprouted** and note the germination rate. From here it's a matter of time — seedlings take roughly 8–14 months to reach first bloom.

## Evaluate and complete

Once seedlings flower, advance to **Blooming** and assess them against the traits you were hoping for. When you're satisfied with your selections, mark the cross as **Completed**.

## Add offspring to your collection

Completing the cross launches the **Add Offspring** flow, pre-filling the pod parent, pollen parent, generation label (F1), and cross reference. Each seedling you want to keep becomes a new plant in your library with full lineage data.
